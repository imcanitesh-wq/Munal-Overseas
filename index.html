About Munal Overseas

A licensed bridge between Nepali workers and global employers.

Munal Overseas Pvt. Ltd. is a government-registered manpower recruitment agency based in Kathmandu, Nepal. We manage the complete recruitment lifecycle — from sourcing and skill verification to documentation, visa processing and deployment — so employers abroad can hire with confidence, and workers can migrate safely and legally.

Named after the Danphe, the Himalayan monal and national bird of Nepal, our name reflects what we do: help Nepali workers rise to new heights, wherever in the world opportunity takes them.

  • Verified & skill-tested candidatesEvery worker is screened, interviewed and trade-tested before being shortlisted to an employer.

  • Zero-cost ethical recruitmentNo hidden recruitment fees are charged to workers, in line with international labour standards.

  • Full legal complianceLabour approval, documentation and visa processing are handled end-to-end under Nepal's Foreign Employment Act.

  • Post-deployment supportOur team stays reachable after arrival to help resolve issues for both worker and employer.

How We Work

Our recruitment process

A clear five-stage pipeline, from your first request to a worker's first day on site.

01

Manpower Request

You share the role, headcount, wage and site requirements with our team.

02

Sourcing & Screening

We shortlist candidates through interviews, document checks and background verification.

03

Trade Testing

Technical and trade roles are skill-tested before being confirmed to the employer.

04

Documentation & Visa

Labour approval, medical checks and visa processing are completed under full legal compliance.

05

Deployment & Follow-up

We arrange travel and orientation, and stay in contact after the worker arrives.
